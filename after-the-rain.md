---
title: After the Rain
date: 2026-11-19
description: A poem about letting the storm pass and finding the courage to begin again.
featured: false
---
The clouds move on, the streets grow still,
the leaves hold drops of silver light;
the world remembers how to breathe
after the long and restless night.

Not every ending means we're lost,
not every pause means we must stay;
sometimes the heart grows new again
in the gentle hush after the rain.
