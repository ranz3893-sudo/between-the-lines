---
title: The Things We Never Say
date: 2026-11-19
description: Some words never leave the heart. They live in pauses, in silence, and in the spaces between two people.
featured: true
---
There are words that never find their way
into the light,

thoughts we carry quietly
into the middle of the night.

There are truths behind a passing glance,
and questions hidden in goodbye;
the sentences we almost speak
then leave beneath a quiet sky.

And somewhere in the things we leave unsaid,
a thousand little truths
are waiting to be read.
