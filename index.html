Poem · Between the Lines

The Things We Never Say

There are words that never find their way into the light, thoughts we carry quietly into the middle of the night. There are truths behind a passing glance, and questions hidden in goodbye; the sentences we almost speak then leave beneath a quiet sky. And somewhere in the things we leave unsaid, a thousand little truths are waiting to be read.

Reflection · Between the Lines

The Quiet Things

We often wait for life to become extraordinary before we allow ourselves to feel grateful. But much of living happens in the ordinary: a familiar voice, a little time to breathe, a meal shared without hurry, a moment of quiet after a difficult day. Perhaps peace is not always something we find. Sometimes it is something we learn to notice. The quiet things are not small because they are easy to miss. They are precious because they are always here, asking us to pay attention.

Poem · Between the Lines

After the Rain

The clouds move on, the streets grow still, the leaves hold drops of silver light; the world remembers how to breathe after the long and restless night. Not every ending means we're lost, not every pause means we must stay; sometimes the heart grows new again in the gentle hush after the rain.

A little about this space

About

A quiet corner of the internet for words that deserve a little room.

Why Between the Lines?

Some feelings are difficult to explain directly. They appear in a pause, a memory, a passing thought, or a line that stays with us long after we have read it. Between the Lines is a space for those feelings.

This website is a personal collection of original poems, reflections, and thoughts about life, love, silence, hope, and the ordinary moments that shape us.

A note to readers

Read at your own pace. Return whenever you need a quiet moment. If a line feels familiar, perhaps it has found the person it was meant for.
