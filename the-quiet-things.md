---
title: The Quiet Things
date: 2026-11-19
description: A reflection on finding meaning in ordinary days.
---
We often wait for life to become extraordinary before we allow ourselves to feel grateful. But much of living happens in the ordinary: a familiar voice, a little time to breathe, a meal shared without hurry, a moment of quiet after a difficult day.

Perhaps peace is not always something we find. Sometimes it is something we learn to notice.

The quiet things are not small because they are easy to miss. They are precious because they are always here, asking us to pay attention.
